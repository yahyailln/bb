A little note

For the one who holds my heart

My love,

I know today felt like the floor dropped out. I was not in the room when it started, and I hate that. I hate that you had to carry the first hour of it without my hand on your back. So I am writing until my hand is tired, because a short "it will be okay" is not enough for a day like this.

If you are reading this with your shoulders up around your ears, you can put the phone down in a minute. You do not have to finish it in one sitting. I will still be here when you come back. I wanted the letter to be long on purpose. I wanted you to have something to walk through, slowly, the way you walk through a house with the lights on after a bad dream.

I keep thinking about the version of you that woke up this morning already braced. The one who checked the time and felt the day sitting on your chest before you even stood up. I know that body. I have watched your shoulders tighten when you make coffee before you've even had a chance to breathe.

I want you to scroll and keep finding me here. That is why it is long. I wanted the letter to last longer than the panic. I wanted there to be more of my voice than of the day that hurt you. If you are still reading, then I am still in the next line.

Imagine us later, when this day is a story we tell with a softer mouth. You will say it was a bad one. I will say we survived it together. I love you, beyond every measure.

Forever yours, ♡
